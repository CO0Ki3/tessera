f32 literal range

naga (Firefox, Servo, Deno) and Tint (Chrome) draw the upper bound of a float literal in different places. naga accepts anything that rounds to a finite f32; Tint accepts only values ≤ f32::MAX. Everything between those two is accepted by one and rejected by the other — and that band contains 3.4028235e38, the decimal people write when they mean the f32 maximum.
